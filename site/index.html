Map zones, from first outline to printed page.

Tools for planning letterbox drops, flyer runs and installs. Whatever KML you open or download in one tool is ready in the next.

  • Your KML files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
  • The last few files you used are kept under Working file, top right.
  • Works with Google Earth and Google My Maps exports.
1BuildOutline suburbs and postcodes as zones 2NumberRenumber zones in a sensible walking order +MergeJoin zones into one outlineAny time 3CountCount the letterboxes in each zone 4PicturesPrint a map page for every zone

Boundaries: ABS (Australia), US Census Bureau, Statistics Canada, LINZ (New Zealand) and OpenStreetMap. Addresses: NSW Spatial Services and OpenStreetMap. Maps © OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenFreeMap and Esri.
