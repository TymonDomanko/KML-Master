Build zones

Pick suburbs or postcodes on the map, group them into zones, then download them as KML.

Boundaries come from OpenStreetMap. Zoom the map to the area you want, then press “Show boundaries here”. What's available varies by country.

    Click areas on the map to add or remove them from the selected zone.
